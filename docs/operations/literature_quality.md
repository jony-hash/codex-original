# Literature Quality Rule

Scope: new scientific claims and material parameters admitted as primary evidence.

- New primary research evidence must normally be published in the current year or
  the two preceding years. As of 2026, this means 2024–2026. Re-evaluate the window
  from the current year for later work.
- Prioritize original research in Nature, Science, Nature Materials, Nature
  Photonics, Nature Nanotechnology, Nature Electronics, Nature Synthesis, Nature
  Communications, Nature Physics, Science Advances, Light: Science & Applications,
  and Advanced Materials. A journal outside this list is not an automatic substitute
  when a listed venue has no matching evidence. Reviews may be used to trace primary
  studies, but are not primary evidence.
- Older sources may remain as provenance for calculations already performed; they
  do not replace recent primary evidence for new scientific claims.
- Bind each material parameter or scientific claim to the exact paper/DOI and source
  location, and record sample composition and morphology, process, phase, layer
  stack, wavelength, temporal conditions, measured quantity, and units when reported.
  Distinguish information unavailable in the retrieved source from information the
  paper does not report.
- A literature-proxy model is permitted when explicitly labelled and tied to its paper
  sample. Do not present proxy values or predicted performance as measurements of the
  project's sputtered Te film or target hardware.
