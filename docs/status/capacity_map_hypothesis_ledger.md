# Capacity-map hypothesis ledger

This ledger is the single mutable index for the diagnostic-only capacity-map
program. Evidence files are immutable; a repaired harness receives an evidence
revision and never a silent architecture-version bump. Rows that describe
O1--O5 are historical preregistration context only: O0 failed and no physical
capacity attribution is currently permitted.

The 2026-08-23 electronic latent-transport branch is also closed at the
pre-optical gate. Its empirical-posterior upper bound localized the remaining
issue to the standard-Gaussian-to-encoded-posterior transport contract, but a
single fixed conditional RealNVP seed did not pass locked semantic recall and
memorization gates. This is an electronic-family failure, not a fiber or
optical-capacity conclusion.

The follow-up class-conditional mixture transport was screened under the same
frozen WAE contract. Its NLL improved over P0, but classwise sliced-distance
reduction was `12.58%` rather than the required `30%`, and the independent
novelty design reached only `0.894` memorization pass fraction at the largest
registered temperature. It therefore closed as
`mixture_latent_fit_failed` before evaluator confirmation; no O0-G or fiber
consumer is authorized.

The prior-native `H-SWAE` lifecycle passed validation, synthetic direction
audit, and CUDA preflight, then validly failed its only seed-8601 screen as
`prior_alignment_fit_failed`. Aggregate SWD reduction was `34.62%`, below the
registered `50%` gate; classes 6 and 7 regressed and the cycle metric worsened.
The route closed before evaluator confirmation, O0-G, or fiber work.

The next registered electronic hypothesis is `H-SCED`: warm-start the same
content-bound WAE v2 seed-7301 weights with a fresh optimizer and replace the
finite-batch quantile-SWD objective with a Gaussian sliced Cramér/energy
U-statistic. Its implementation, config, tests, and `validate` manifest are
registered, but no valid validation evidence or CUDA result exists yet.
Only clean lifecycle validation is currently permitted; formal promotion,
optical training, and fiber training remain false.

## v2.5 WAE electronic O0 correction and valid stop (2026-08-22)

The earlier WAE-MMD O0 record was invalidated because reconstruction decoded
random prior codes and several acceptance metrics were placeholders; the
correction capsule is `experiments/archive/v2_wae_o0_v1_invalid_harness/`.
The corrected v2 contract used encoded-latent reconstruction, full-coverage
balancing, deterministic resume, independent reference metrics, true shuffled
conditioning, official 10,000-test generation, JVP Jacobians, and chunked
memorization. It passed validation and preflight on clean commits and used no
evaluator in calibration or training.

The corrected seed-7301 confirm is a valid scientific failure: locked-panel
primary accuracy `0.930–0.952`, secondary accuracy `0.826–0.852`, secondary
minimum recall `0.36–0.44`, agreement `0.828–0.856`, and official-test
secondary accuracy/minimum recall `0.8374/0.3683`. Camera fraction was about
`0.637`, shuffled-label primary accuracy `0.090–0.102`, all 30 code Jacobians
had rank 8, and deterministic replay passed. Thus the electronic image
manifold still does not satisfy the dual-evaluator gate; this is not evidence
of an optical or fiber bottleneck.

The precise classification is `wae_image_manifold_failed`; the fixed next
action is `close_fiber_restart`. The gate stopped before seeds 7302/7303,
locked-final, O0-G, and P100/P196/P400. The route remains
`training_authorized=false`, `formal_promotion=false`, and
`synthetic_diagnostic_only=true`; no extra epochs, seed substitution, or
fiber-aware training is authorized.

## v2.3-r2 correction boundary

Revision-2 D0 evidence is archived as `invalid_harness` under
`experiments/archive/v2_3_r2_invalid_harness/`. The implementation lacked a
seed-bound model initialization contract and did not step the auxiliary
classifier/Q heads after their gradients were computed. Therefore the observed
code-fraction decline is not evidence for feature-matching dominance, objective
conflict, or generator-capacity failure. The only established fact is the
training implementation defect. Locked-final, C0--C3, optical branches, and
cross-dataset tests were not executed. v2.4 tests these update boundaries only.

The single v2.4 CUDA probe passed preflight and then validly failed its first
health gate as `discriminator_identifiability_failure`: real auxiliary
classifier accuracy improved from 0.10 to 0.26, while Q latent error did not
improve by the required 10%. This closes v2.4 without a D0 reachability or
generator-capacity claim; no command candidate or optical branch is authorized.

## v2.3 electronic-oracle preregistration

The branch `experiment/v2-o0-feasibility-v23` is a new, research-only contract
based on the immutable v2.2 closeout. Its sole question is whether a teacher-free
single-forward electronic amplitude-command generator can pass the qualified
dual-evaluator protocol before any optical work is reconsidered. The ordered
counterfactuals are D0 (28x28 image upper bound), C0 (current 8D exact anchor),
C1 (higher-capacity 8D exact anchor), C2 (higher-capacity 8D soft anchor), and
C3 (32D soft diagnostic upper bound). D0 failure stops the branch; C3 can never
authorize physical follow-up.

Implementation and CUDA preflight passed, but the revision-2 D0 probe is retained
only as `invalid_harness`: model initialization was not bound to the evidence
seed and the auxiliary classifier/Q heads were not stepped by their optimizer.
Its observed code-fraction decline therefore cannot be called a valid model
failure. Smoke, confirm, locked-final, command candidates, and all optical
branches were stopped and must not be inferred from that artifact. The later
v2.4 probe is the only valid follow-up and closed at
`discriminator_identifiability_failure`.
