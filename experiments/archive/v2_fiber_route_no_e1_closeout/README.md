# v2 Synthetic Fiber Route: Closed, No E1

This immutable capsule records the final synthetic, diagnostic-only fiber
attribution chain as of 2026-08-22. Its consumer is the current v2 handoff
and the future research-authorization gate. The rejection decision is fixed:
no fiber training, no E1, no modal regularizer, and no formal promotion.

The reverse necessity check passed before archival. The existing valid
evidence is sufficient to make that decision, so this closeout performs no
CUDA measurement, model forward, optimizer construction, evaluator comparison,
or training. It does not modify the original `outputs/` runs.

The capsule contains only JSON decision evidence and its index. Checkpoints,
datasets, images, large field artifacts, and failed runs remain in their
original read-only `outputs/` locations and are intentionally not copied.

The route may be reconsidered only after a new electronic O0 hypothesis is
independently qualified and passes the restart contract in
`fiber_route_closeout_summary.json`.
