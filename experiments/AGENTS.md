# Experiments

`experiments/archive/` is historical reference material, not an active
entrypoint or acceptance authority. Keep its files intact. New current research
belongs in `scripts/research/`, `configs/research/`, and
`tests/research/`, grouped by the same subject family.

Do not move or rewrite `outputs/`, `data/raw/`, `.tmp/`, or legacy experiment
artifacts while reorganizing code. See the root [`AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md) for
artifact and research-run rules.
